A Beginning

Why write about spiritual things in a public space?
February 25, 2026 · 1 min · 89 words · Tyler

There is something vulnerable about writing on matters of the spirit. These are the quieter rooms of a life — less subject to the metrics of productivity, harder to demonstrate or prove.

And yet here we are.

This section of the blog is for honest reflection: on faith, on contemplation, on the slow work of becoming more fully human. Some posts will be personal. Some will engage ideas from theology, philosophy, or the contemplative traditions. All of them will be attempts at thinking carefully and living well.
